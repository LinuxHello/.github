# Security policy

This policy applies to every repository of the LinuxHello organisation that has no `SECURITY.md` of its own. The full policy, including the security model and response times, is in [`linux-hello`'s SECURITY.md](https://github.com/LinuxHello/linux-hello/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

> **Status: pre-alpha.** Nothing here authenticates anyone yet. Do not use it to protect real accounts.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting** in `linux-hello`: open its [Security tab](https://github.com/LinuxHello/linux-hello/security), then **Report a vulnerability**. Use it for weaknesses anywhere in the organisation, including the architecture or a decision record that would weaken a defence.

Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion, and do not post details anywhere public before a fix is out.

Acknowledgement within 7 days, first assessment within 14 days, fix or mitigation within 90 days of the report.
